On August 14, 2026, the INPI published its Strategic Plan for the 2027-2036 period — the first ten-year planning cycle of the authority, approved by the Internal Governance Committee (CGI) in July 2026. The document replaces the 2023-2026 four-year cycle and defines seven Strategic Objectives that will guide the Institute's actions over the next ten years.

For companies, startups, and IP offices, the plan matters for three reasons: it signals the directions for patent and trademark examination, announces new services (such as IP asset valuation), and defines regulatory priorities that may reach the Industrial Property Law (LPI). This guide explains what the plan contains, objective by objective, and what changes in practice.

## What is the 2027-2036 Strategic Plan

The Strategic Plan is INPI's foundational governance instrument: it aligns senior management's long-term vision with tactical-operational deliverables. The previous cycle (2023-2026) was four-year; the new plan inaugurates a ten-year horizon, with a **guiding and less prescriptive** character — instead of prescribing activities, it defines the organization's major strategic drivers.

The drafting process was participatory. The Extended Workshop, held between May 12 and 14, 2026, brought together 134 participants — president, directors, coordinators, and staff from all general coordinations. Methodological conduct was led by the Public Management Innovation Laboratory (Pólen) of ENSP/Fiocruz, combining design thinking, behavioral sciences (BASIC methodology, with ENAP's SIMPLES MENTE deck), and change management. The full document is available on the [INPI Strategic Planning page](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/governanca/planejamento-estrategico/plano-estrategico).

## The new planning model

The plan establishes a three-level planning architecture:

| Level | Instrument | Horizon | Monitoring |
|-------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| Strategic | Strategic Plan | 10 years (2027-2036) | Annual, with formal evaluation in Year 5 (2031) |
| Tactical | Business Plan | 4 years | Monthly |
| Operational | Action Plan | 12 months | Monthly |

The deployment uses the *catchball* methodology (bidirectional top-down and bottom-up flow), and there is a planned overlap mechanism: in 2031, INPI will already begin preparing the next plan (2032-2041), avoiding planning gaps.

## Mission, Vision, and Values

The plan reaffirms the Mission of "protecting and promoting Intellectual Property as a stimulus to innovation for the sustainable development of Brazil" and the Vision of consolidating itself as a world reference — a world-class office. The Values were reformulated into six pillars: commitment to the public interest, humanized management and valuing talent, reliability and legal certainty, integrity and impartiality, operational excellence, and innovation with purpose.

## The 7 Strategic Objectives

| # | Objective | What it means in practice |
|---|-----------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Achieve world-class productivity and quality in examination | Process management, AI and automation, integrated data architecture, harmonization between 1st and 2nd instances |
| 2 | Value people | Restructuring the IP career, attracting and retaining talent, workforce sizing |
| 3 | Strengthen governance and management | Institutional redesign (State nature), budget sustainability, unification of filing systems |
| 4 | Popularize the use of IP | IP in Schools Program, mass communication, regional presence in the five regions of Brazil |
| 5 | Expand international leadership | South-South cooperation, presence at WIPO, regional processing in MERCOSUR |
| 6 | Stimulate the monetization of intangible assets | IP valuation service, IP as bank collateral, certification of appraisers |
| 7 | Promote a balanced regulatory environment | Fight against counterfeiting (including e-commerce), conflict mediation, improvement of the LPI |

Each objective is aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) — mainly SDG 8 (decent work and economic growth), 9 (industry, innovation, and infrastructure), and 16 (effective institutions).

## What changes in practice for companies and users

Some strategies in the plan have a direct impact on those who file patents and trademarks in Brazil:

**1. Unification of systems and AI in service (Objective 3).** The plan provides for "promoting the unification of systems used for filing and maintaining assets," with plain language, AI, and automatic alerts about obligations. This follows the path of the [Patent Services Module]({{< relref "posts/inpi-modulo-servicos-patentes-novas-funcionalidades-2026/" >}}) and the [INPI 4.0 automation plan]({{< relref "posts/inpi-automation-roadmap-2025-2029/" >}}), which have been unifying patent examination since 2025.

**2. Administrative court of second instance (Objective 1).** The plan provides for structuring a second-instance administrative court — the evolution of INPI's appellate model, with greater robustness and specialization in collegiate decisions. This should reduce decisional divergences and, potentially, the judicialization of the Institute's acts.

**3. IP asset valuation (Objective 6).** INPI intends to implement an intellectual property asset valuation service, with guidelines, governance, and practical tools (including valuation calculators). The objective is to serve as a market reference in assignment contracts, licensing, and financing operations secured by IP assets.

**4. IP as bank collateral (Objective 6).** The plan provides for articulating with financial regulators and banks to create mechanisms so that IP assets are recognized as collateral in credit operations — expanding access to financing for knowledge-intensive companies.

**5. Conflict mediation (Objective 7).** INPI wants to establish mediation and conciliation mechanisms in the first and second administrative instances, promoting "the culture of coexistence in replacement of the culture of litigation."

**6. Improvement of the LPI (Objective 7).** The plan provides for INPI's active contribution to improvements in Law No. 9,279/1996, focusing on agility, the digital era, and international best practices — in line with the [30 years of LPI]({{< relref "posts/lpi-30-anos-propostas-aperfeicoamento-inpi/" >}}) report, which has already mapped modernization proposals.

**7. Fight against counterfeiting in e-commerce (Objective 7).** The regulation of the improper use of IP assets in e-commerce formally enters the strategy, with extrajudicial notifications, opinions, and partnerships with enforcement agencies.

## The context: budget sustainability

A cross-cutting theme of the plan is the authority's financial sustainability. INPI advocates the "full and continuous allocation of resources collected by the Institute itself for reinvestment in modernization and service delivery" — and Strategy 3.4 provides for defending an institutional redesign that preserves the nature of an exclusive State institution, expanding administrative and financial autonomy. It is the same debate that reached the Judiciary in the [TRF-2 decision on INPI's restructuring]({{< relref "posts/trf2-decisao-abpi-fortalecimento-inpi/" >}}).

## What to watch from here on

The plan is guiding — the actual execution will come from the annual Action Plans and the four-year Business Plan, with monthly monitoring and a formal mid-cycle evaluation in 2031. Points to track for those who use IP in Brazil:

- The implementation of the second-instance administrative court and its impact on appeal deadlines;
- The launch of the asset valuation service and the regulation of IP as bank collateral;
- Concrete proposals to amend the LPI sent to Congress;
- The speed of unification of electronic systems (patents, trademarks, industrial designs).
