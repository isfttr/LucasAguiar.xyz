Every grant, registration, or communication from the INPI is published in a single official medium: the **Industrial Property Gazette (RPI)**. If you have filed a patent, registered a trademark, or are processing a geographical indication, sooner or later you will need to locate yourself in the RPI to find out about the publication, subsidies, oppositions, or grant. This guide explains how it is organized, when it is released, and how to search for your process.

## What is the RPI

The RPI is the official gazette of the National Institute of Industrial Property. It is the means by which INPI publicly communicates its acts: granting of trademarks, registration of patents, industrial designs, geographical indications, technology contracts, registration of computer programs and integrated circuit topographies, in addition to general communications about the institute's operations.

The publication regime is established by [Resolution nº 22/2013, of March 18, 2013](https://revistas.inpi.gov.br/). Since 01/31/2017 (RPI 2404), the Gazette has ceased to be published as a single volume and is now published **divided into sections corresponding to INPI services**, which makes it easier to consult only the relevant part.

> In September 2026, the RPI is at number **2906** (09/15/2026); number 2905 (09/08/2026) had a correction: the Communications Section was republished, and the Industrial Designs section was updated. These adjustments are normal, and it is necessary to know how to detect them (see further below).

## Where and How Often It's Published

The RPI is published on the INPI's journals website:

- **Website:** [https://revistas.inpi.gov.br/](https://revistas.inpi.gov.br/)
- **Frequency:** weekly. In 2026, it is released **every Tuesday** (check the date indicated in each issue).
- **Access:** free and open, no gov.br account or PAG required.

Each issue groups several independent files — one per section — in PDF format and, for those who automate, TXT/XML.

## RPI Sections (issue 2906)

Each section corresponds to a type of act or service:

| Section | Content | Format |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Communications** | General notices from INPI (public notices, deadlines, ordinances, agendas) | PDF |
| **Trademarks** | Granting, extension, lapsing, and oppositions of trademarks | PDF + XML |
| **Patents** | Invention patents and utility models: filings, grants, subsidies, nullities | PDF + TXT |
| **Industrial Designs** | Registration of industrial designs (3D protection provided by LPI) | PDF |
| **Geographical Indications** | Recognitions of GIs (indications of provenance and denominations of origin) | PDF |
| **Technology Contracts** | Registration of technology transfer contracts | PDF + TXT |
| **Computer Program** | Software registration (computer programs) | PDF + TXT |
| **Integrated Circuit Topography** | Registration of integrated circuit topographies | PDF |

Each file is named with the pattern `<Sección><NúmeroRevista>.pdf`, for example `Patentes2906.pdf` or `Marcas2906.pdf`, and is located at `https://revistas.inpi.gov.br/pdf/<Sección><Número>.pdf`.

## What TXT/XML files are for

Along with PDFs, INPI offers simplified plain text and XML versions of some sections (Patents, Trademarks, Technology Contracts, Computer Program), packaged in `.zip`:

- They are used to **search by case number or name without reading the PDF**.
- They allow integrating publications into proprietary applications or spreadsheets (e.g., monitoring the grant of a client's patent).
- They are the basis of many RPI consultation systems.

> ⚠️ INPI clarifies that these formats "contain simplified RPI information, for use through applications". For legal purposes and exact dates, always check the official PDF.

## How to find your process in the RPI

The practical, step-by-step approach:

1. **Identify the section** according to the act (patent → Patents section; trademark → Trademarks section; GI → Geographical Indications section).
2. **Download the PDF** of the gazette issue corresponding to the date of the act.
3. **Search by case number or name** of the owner (Ctrl+F): each published application has a process number (e.g., format `BR xx 2026 000000-0`).
4. **Verify the deadlines** that appear with the publication: subsidies, opposition, or appeal. If you do not meet them, you lose the instance.

## Why it's important not to lose sight of the RPI

The RPI is not just another step: it is **where the deadline runs**. That's why it's worth monitoring it even if you have an active case:

- The **publication of a patent filing** enables third parties to submit observations and oppositions within a deadline counted from that publication.
- The **granting** of a trademark or patent becomes public in the RPI; from then on, deadlines run for paying fees and annuities.
- **Corrections** are frequent (a section may be republished or updated in another issue, as happened with 2905). Always compare the most recent issue of your section.

## Final Tips

- **Save the direct link** to your section: only the number at the end of the URL changes (`Comunicados2906.pdf` → `Comunicados2907.pdf`).
- **Also download the TXT/XML file** if you need to process data in volume.
- **Detect republications**: if an issue has been "republished" or a section "updated," the valid version is the one in the most recent notice — check the updated PDF before asserting a deadline.

The RPI is the source of truth for INPI. Mastering its consultation is one of the most underestimated — and most profitable — competencies in industrial property practice in Brazil.
