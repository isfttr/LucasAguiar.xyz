INPI is in the final stretch of migrating patent services to the new Patent Services Module. The homologation platform — the test environment open to users — is available until **August 14, 2026**. As of **August 19**, filing patent applications will be done **exclusively** through the new module, with the Electronic Filing system (service 200) retired for this purpose.

For those who file patents in Brazil — independent inventors, startups, IP firms, and intellectual property departments — this is an operational turning point. This guide covers, in practice, what the test environment is, how to access it, how to request credentials, and what to do to get through the transition without missing deadlines.

## What is the Patent Services Module

The module is the centerpiece of [INPI's automation plan]({{< relref "posts/inpi-automation-roadmap-2025-2029/" >}}), the so-called INPI 4.0. The idea is to replace fragmented systems — filing, GRU generation, case tracking, and annuity management — with a single integrated platform, with a personalized workspace and contextual user guidance.

The [second version of the module]({{< relref "posts/inpi-modulo-servicos-patentes-novas-funcionalidades-2026/" >}}), announced in July, brought two structural changes: filing of national applications (invention patents, utility models, and addition certificates) and entry into the PCT national phase must now be done **only** through the module, and annuities are now managed within the platform itself, with deadline notifications in the user's mailbox.

## The 2026 migration schedule

| Period | Milestone |
|---------|-------|
| **Jul 27 to Aug 14** | Homologation platform open for user testing |
| **Aug 19** | Exclusive use of the module for filing patent applications (end of service 200 for this purpose) |
| **November 2026** | Third version, with migration of remaining patent services |
| **By end of 2026** | All patent services available in the module |

The test period ends Aug 14 — those who haven't accessed the homologation environment have a few days to familiarize themselves before mandatory use.

## How to access the test platform (homologation)

The homologation environment is hosted at a separate address from the production system:

**`https://hom-portalpat.inpi.gov.br/home`**

Unlike the final system, which will be accessed with a gov.br account, the test environment requires its own credentials. Step by step:

1. **Access the Patent Services Module** (production system) and open a ticket in **User Support**.
2. **Request creation of a test user** for the homologation platform.
3. **Wait for processing** — INPI provides access credentials after reviewing the ticket.
4. **Go to `hom-portalpat.inpi.gov.br`** with the credentials received and explore the features.

INPI provides supporting materials and tutorials on the platform itself, in the **Manuals and Tutorials** section. It's worth going through them before testing: the interface is new and the navigation logic differs from the Electronic Filing system.

### Caution with sensitive data

An important notice from INPI: the homologation environment is for **testing** — the data entered there is not real and serves only to validate features. **Do not enter sensitive data or confidential information** into the test platform, including client data, confidential documents, or strategic commercial information. Use fictitious data to simulate the workflows.

## What changes on August 19

As of Aug 19, filing patent applications will be done exclusively through the Patent Services Module. In practice:

- **Electronic Filing (service 200) will no longer accept filings** of invention patents, utility models, and addition certificates, nor entry into the PCT national phase.
- The **PAG system remains available** for issuing payment slips during the transition period.
- Other filings (responses to office actions, appeals, examination requests, etc.) follow the third-version schedule in November.

For those with filing deadlines in late August, the recommendation is simple: **don't leave it to the last day**. Make your first filing through the module before Aug 19 — in the production environment, after activation — or test the full workflow in homologation while it is open, to reduce the chance of error at the critical moment.

## Checklist for the transition

- [ ] Create a gov.br account with the required security level (access to the production module uses the gov.br account)
- [ ] Open a ticket in User Support requesting test credentials
- [ ] Test in homologation: filing an application, generating GRU, tracking a case
- [ ] Review the Manuals and Tutorials available on the platform
- [ ] Validate with your team/firm the internal workflows that depend on service 200
- [ ] Check that integrations of IP management systems with INPI are updated for the new module
- [ ] Map filing and annuity deadlines in the weeks after Aug 19 and anticipate as much as possible

## Impact for each user profile

### Individual inventors and startups

The module reduces the learning curve with assisted completion and contextual guidance — fewer formality errors, which today are one of the biggest causes of office actions and delays. Annuities integrated with deadline notifications help those who don't have their own portfolio management system.

### IP firms

The migration requires reviewing internal workflows. Firms that automate filing need to update integrations and train teams before Aug 19. The testing period is the window to identify bottlenecks without risking real filings.

### Companies with international portfolios

Entering the PCT national phase through the module unifies the process with national filing, eliminating the need for separate systems. For those with applications entering the national phase in the second half of the year, it's worth mapping the 30-month deadlines and checking the new workflow in advance.

## What to watch for in the coming months

- **Aug 14:** end of the homologation testing period — last chance to test before mandatory use
- **Aug 19:** start of exclusive use of the module for patent filing
- **November:** third version of the module, with migration of remaining patent services
- **Announcements in RPI:** INPI usually publishes guidance and possible schedule adjustments in Section I — worth monitoring

The modernization of the Brazilian patent system is a process that has been under construction since the [2025-2029 automation plan]({{< relref "posts/inpi-automation-roadmap-2025-2029/" >}}). Aug 19 marks the first major mandatory milestone in this path: those who prepare now avoid last-minute scrambling.
