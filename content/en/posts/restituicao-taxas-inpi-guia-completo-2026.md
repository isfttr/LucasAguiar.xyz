Paying the wrong fee to INPI (Brazil's patent and trademark office) is more common than it looks: wrong GRU code, overpayment, duplicate payment, or a deposit that never became a filing. The good news is that INPI refunds those amounts — as long as you follow the rules of [Resolution INPI No. 204/2017](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/marcas/arquivos/legislacao/RES_204_2017.pdf) (in Portuguese).

This guide covers the cases where a refund is available, the full step-by-step electronic filing process, required documents, deadlines, and what to do if your request receives an office action or is rejected.

## When INPI fee refunds are available

Article 4 of Resolution INPI No. 204/2017 lists eight refund scenarios:

| # | Scenario | Practical example |
|---|----------|-------------------|
| I | Service for which the applicant never filed the petition | Paid the GRU but never filed the trademark or patent application |
| II | Service paid twice | Two different GRUs paid for the same service |
| III | Same GRU paid twice | The same fee slip paid two times |
| IV | Overpayment | Paid without the micro-enterprise or small-business discount you were entitled to |
| V | Reimbursement under the PCT | Cases set out in the Patent Cooperation Treaty |
| VI | Service paid with the wrong code | Wrong fee code used when generating the GRU |
| VII | Service with wrong application number | GRU issued with an application number that does not match the service |
| VIII | Underpayment without topping up | Insufficient amount paid and never complemented |

Important: the refund request itself is **free of charge**. The GRU generated for the refund petition comes with a zero value.

## Step by step: how to request a refund

The official procedure (published on the [INPI fee refund page](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/restituicao-de-retribuicao), in Portuguese) has two stages: generating the zero-value GRU and filing the petition.

### Stage 1 — Generate the zero-value GRU (code 801)

1. Go to the [INPI portal](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br) and, under "Quick Access", choose any service (trademarks, patents, industrial designs, etc.)
2. Click "Costs and Payment" and then "GRU Issuance System"
3. Log in with your gov.br account
4. Select the unit "Administração Geral" (General Administration)
5. Enter code **801** and confirm
6. Select the GRU you want refunded and confirm — the system will show zero value
7. Finalize and note the "Our Number" with code 801 (starts with five zeros followed by twelve digits)

### Stage 2 — Electronic filing

1. Access the [electronic filing module](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/restituicao-de-retribuicao/peticionamento-eletronico)
2. Log in
3. Enter the "Our Number" with code 801 from the previous stage
4. Fill in the form and attach the required documents
5. Submit the petition

**Note:** you must open a separate process for each GRU. It is not possible to request refunds for multiple fee slips in a single petition.

## Required documents

Article 7 of Resolution 204/2017 sets out the documents:

- **Payment receipt of the GRU** related to the undue payment — mandatory in all cases. For duplicate payments, attach receipts for both slips or state both payment dates in the justification field
- **Power of attorney with express powers to receive and give receipt** — if the bank account in the form is not in the name of the GRU holder. If the account belongs to the attorney's firm, you must also prove the link between the attorney and the beneficiary
- **Company constitutional instrument** — instead of a power of attorney, when the account is in the name of the managing partner (or majority partner, if there is none) of the legal entity that holds the GRU
- **Qualification declaration** — if you were entitled to a discount (micro-enterprise, small business) but paid without it, attach a declaration proving your category

## Deadlines you need to know

- **5 years** — maximum deadline to request a refund, counted from the GRU payment date
- **30 calendar days** — deadline to comply with office actions, counted from the email notification
- **15 business days** — INPI's deadline to pay the refund after the approval is published in the RPI
- **Up to 7 days** — response time of the "Fale Conosco" channel (subject "Financeiro") for inquiries about requests not yet published

Refund requests are always published in the RPI (the official gazette), in the "Comunicados" (Notices) section. Note that approved and rejected lists are published separately.

## Special situations

**Federal SIAFI system users:** they have no bank account, so they must fill the account field with "99999999" and inform in the justification field their bank, branch and UG/GESTÃO codes.

**Rejection:** every applicant has the right to appeal. Instructions are published in the same rejection decision, always in the RPI.

**Office action:** INPI notifies by email (the address provided in the petition), with instructions and a 30-calendar-day deadline. Monitor the email until the process is concluded.

## What to watch in 2026

In August 2026, INPI published in [RPI 2900 (Section I)](https://revistas.inpi.gov.br/pdf/Comunicados2900.pdf) a notice about instabilities in the fee refund filing system that occurred since the beginning of the year. Users with cases on the published list must access the filing system again, enter the generated GRU number and download the document — a reminder that double-checking your protocol and payment receipts is essential.

For anyone who pays INPI fees regularly, the workflow is worth the care: always note the GRU number, keep the receipts, and confirm the petition was actually filed. When a mistake happens, the refund path is open — and it is free.
