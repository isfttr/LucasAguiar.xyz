On October 8, 2026, INPI made available the last six editions of the series of **15 Intellectual Property Guides for Business**, concluding a project initiated in 2024 in partnership with the Intellectual Property Office of Singapore (IPOS). The result is official, free material in Portuguese that covers the entire lifecycle of an IP strategy: from identifying the assets you already own to monetization, financing, and defense against infringers.

This article is a curated index of the complete series, with the theme of each guide and suggestions on how to use them according to your business phase. It is a starting point for anyone who wants to understand intellectual property from a business perspective — without relying on paid courses.

## What is the IP Guides for Business series

The series is the result of a partnership between INPI and the **Intellectual Property Office of Singapore (IPOS)**, which holds the authorship of the original publications, adapted by the Institute for the Brazilian reality. The guides were launched between 2024 and 2026, with the last six editions published in October 2026.

Unlike INPI's technical manuals (focused on filing, examinations, and procedures), the Guides for Business have a **strategic and managerial** approach: they treat IP as a business asset, not as a bureaucratic process. Therefore, they serve entrepreneurs and SMEs as well as innovation managers in larger companies.

All are available for free on the [INPI's intellectual property guides for business page](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/central-de-conteudo/publicacoes-de-propriedade-industrial/guias-de-propriedade-industrial-para-negocios/). The conclusion of the series was announced by INPI in its [Content Hub](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/central-de-conteudo/noticias/inpi-conclui-a-serie-de-15-guias-de-pi-para-negocios/).

## How to use the guides according to your business phase

The series does not need to be read in numerical order. It is organized around the IP lifecycle within a company:

**Phase 1 — Understand and map assets (Guides 1, 2, 4, and 5).** For those starting out, Guides 1 and 2 teach how to recognize and generate IP assets; Guides 4 and 5 show how to organize the portfolio and conduct an audit to identify what is actually worth protecting.

**Phase 2 — Protect and monitor the market (Guides 6, 8, and 9).** Guide 6 connects IP to competitive intelligence — patent searching and competitor monitoring. Guide 8 helps turn protection into commercial strategy, and Guide 9, to manage risks before they become problems.

**Phase 3 — Monetize and grow (Guides 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15).** This is the most valuable part for those who already have registered assets: economically evaluating IP (11), using it as collateral for financing (12), structuring tax benefits (13), generating revenue with licensing and franchising (14), and strengthening the brand (15).

**Phase 4 — Defend and operate beyond borders (Guides 3, 7, and 10).** Collaborations with third parties (3), international expansion (7), and responding to infringers or accusations (10) close the cycle.

## Why the series is worthwhile in practice

Three reasons make the IP Guides for Business a resource different from other INPI materials:

1.  **Official and free.** It is produced (and adapted) by the Institute itself, at no cost and in Portuguese — contrasting with the abundance of paid content of questionable quality on IP topics.
2.  **Managerial focus.** The series treats IP as a business strategy, covering gaps that technical filing manuals do not address — especially in the valuation, financing, and tax benefits guides.
3.  **Coherence with the trend of IP as an asset.** Guide 12, for example, directly aligns with the movement to use [patents and trademarks as credit collateral in Brazil]({{< relref "posts/patentes-marcas-garantia-credito-brasil-guia/" >}}), an increasingly relevant topic for startups and innovation companies.

## What to observe

The conclusion of the series in 2026 reinforces INPI's strategy to expand access to IP knowledge for the productive sector — a movement already seen in the [Technological Radars]({{< relref "posts/radares-tecnologicos-inpi-guia-completo/" >}}). For the entrepreneur, the gain is a single and official point of guidance, from concept to monetization.

It's worth following two things: whether INPI will expand the series with new thematic guides (the material mentions the possibility of sectoral versions) and how the evaluation and financing guides (11, 12, and 13) will be absorbed by banks and development programs. Those who want to start can find help in Guide 1 — and those who have already protected assets gain more from Guides 11 to 15.
