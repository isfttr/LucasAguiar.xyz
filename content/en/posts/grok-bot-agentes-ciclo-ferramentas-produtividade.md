In August 2026, xAI launched [Grok Bot](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot) in beta: a team of "always-on" AI agents, each with its own cloud computer, that subscribe to the tools you already use, perform end-to-end tasks, and talk to each other in threads — a "chief of staff" bot coordinating inbox, expenses, recruiting, or bug-fixing specialists. In the official presentation, it looks like the future of work.

But I've seen this movie before. A few years ago, the same enthusiasm surrounded Obsidian, Notion, and Todoist: each one presented itself as the definitive hub of your digital life, with plugins, automations, and a passionate community. None of them disappeared, but they stopped being the center — the productivity market fragmented again, and what was "the platform" became a feature inside other platforms.

The risk of Grok Bot is not technical; it's strategic. If the pattern repeats, within a few months OpenAI, Google, or Microsoft will incorporate "a team of agents that talk to each other" as just another feature — and whoever migrated their real workflow to Grok Bot is left with the rework of switching tools again, rebuilding routines, and relearning. Worse: you give a third-party company access to your accounts, emails, and CRM, and what the bots learned about the way you work stays locked in a proprietary system over which you have no control — the same [hidden cost of data]({{< relref "posts/modelos-ia-abertos-vs-fronteira-dados-2026/" >}}) we've already discussed here.

None of this means Grok Bot is bad — specialized agents with their own computer are a genuine advance. But the question worth asking before adopting it is the same one we should have asked with productivity tools: does this solve a problem that will still exist two years from now, or am I just jumping to the tool of the moment? Today's AI market pattern is yesterday's productivity tools pattern: lots of promise, quick incorporation by competitors, and oblivion. In the end, sometimes it makes more sense to wait and receive the implementation in what you already use. The cost of incorporating these features is very low.
